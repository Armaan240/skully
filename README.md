# skully
a darked theme Halloween keychain , with mechanical fidget at back , and can be printed separately and could be joined with glue , its with a custom keycap and and can used to click thousand times :D
[checkout pintables for fun :P](https://www.printables.com/model/1877165-halloween-keychain)

## how i can make myself a one ?
oki nice questions ,
1.first thing first you gotta need a 3d printed to print part ! , and some filament (you can use same filament color ) but i recommend using the og orange and black
2.after printing them get all the part printed , get that big bar and base and paste with glue around the outline 
<img width="385" height="182" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/07d79e54-10e9-4ad8-bc7c-5bb57a658a5f" />
3.then get that orange part and the house and paste them together and paste on the base and paste all those birds and small details !
<img width="425" height="237" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/80e09968-e6eb-4238-adc2-ee899cd67abf" />
4.flip the keychain and get that box thingi put some glue in it and fix a key switch (cherry MX style) and get the keycap and put into , make sure you have a small size mx key otherwise the key caps wont fit , so you have to make hole to get into it ! after that paste the skull with glue :D
<img width="156" height="162" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/46e11a1a-ac62-4b6e-a77e-6f521bb799b9" />
5. now the last part (optional) you can get those orange parts letters and paste them i space , i didnt pasted cuz it looks cool as it is :D
**for parts and budget you need to build , refer**:- https://github.com/Armaan240/skully/blob/main/BOM.csv
